Acme Cloud — Product Guide

Product, pricing, security and FAQ · Version 2026.3

1. Product overview

Acme Cloud is a customer analytics platform for B2B software companies. It connects product usage, billing and CRM data into one live model of every account, user and dollar, then turns it into dashboards, smart alerts and AI-powered insights.

Teams use Acme Cloud to spot churn risk earlier, find expansion revenue, and stop rebuilding the same reports in spreadsheets every week.

Who it is for: revenue operations, customer success, product and finance teams at B2B SaaS companies, from 20-person startups to enterprises with thousands of employees.

2. Core features

Ask your data

Ask questions in plain language (for example "Which accounts are at churn risk this quarter?") and get the number, the chart and the list of accounts behind it. Every answer shows the underlying metric definition and data source.

Live revenue dashboards

Ready-made dashboards for MRR, ARR, net revenue retention, expansion, contraction, churn and cohort retention. Dashboards update continuously as events arrive, with no SQL required.

Customer health scores

A health score for every account, combining product usage, support tickets and billing signals. Health weights can be customised on the Growth and Enterprise plans.

Smart alerts

Get notified in Slack, Microsoft Teams or email when a key account's usage drops, a trial converts, a payment fails or usage spikes. Available on the Growth and Enterprise plans.

AI insights

Weekly AI-written summaries explain what changed in your metrics and why, with links to the accounts driving the change. Available on the Growth and Enterprise plans.

Governed metrics

Every metric is defined once in a shared metrics layer and every query is logged, so finance, sales and product work from the same numbers.

3. How it works

  1. Connect — plug in your data sources in minutes, with no engineering project.
  2. Model — Acme Cloud automatically unifies accounts, users and revenue across sources and keeps them current.
  3. Act — dashboards, alerts and AI insights your whole team can trust.

Data syncs every 15 minutes on Starter and in near real time (under 1 minute) on Growth and Enterprise.

4. Integrations

Native connectors: Stripe, Chargebee, Salesforce, HubSpot, Segment, Snowflake, BigQuery, PostgreSQL, Zendesk, Intercom and Slack. Product events can also be sent through the Acme Cloud JavaScript and Python SDKs or the REST events API. Dashboards can be exported to Google Sheets and CSV. A warehouse is not required, but if you have Snowflake or BigQuery, Acme Cloud can read from it directly.

9. Frequently asked questions

How is Acme Cloud different from a BI tool?

BI tools need a data team to model everything first. Acme Cloud ships with a ready-made model of accounts, users and revenue, so you get answers on day one.

Do I need a data warehouse?

No. Connect your tools directly. If you already have Snowflake or BigQuery, Acme Cloud can read from it too.

Is my data used to train shared AI models?

No. Your data and questions are never used to train models shared across customers.

How long does setup take?

Most teams see their first live dashboard the same day they connect a data source.

Book a 30-minute walkthrough with our team from the website, or ask the assistant to connect you with sales.
