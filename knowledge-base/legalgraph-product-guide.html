LegalGraph AI — Product Guide

Product, pricing, security and FAQ · Version 2026.3

1. Product overview

LegalGraph AI is a legal knowledge graph for in-house legal and compliance teams. It maps contracts, case law and regulations into one connected graph, so teams research faster, spot risk earlier, and get every answer with a citation back to the exact source clause, case or regulation.

Unlike a document search tool, which only finds documents, LegalGraph maps the relationships between clauses, cases and regulations, so an answer can explain why and show the exact source it came from.

Who it is for: legal and compliance teams at mid-market and enterprise companies (typically 5 to 500 legal professionals), plus law firms that manage large contract portfolios for clients.

2. Core features

Graph-powered research

Ask questions in plain language and get answers linked to the exact clause, case or regulation they come from — never a bare summary. Example: "What is our standard liability cap?" returns the playbook rule and the contracts that fall below it.

Contract intelligence

Extracts obligations, key dates, renewal and termination terms, and risks across the whole contract portfolio, and compares every contract against your playbook automatically. Deviations are flagged with the clause reference (for example, a liability cap below the playbook minimum).

Playbook automation

Encode your negotiation playbook (standard positions, fallback positions and red lines) once; LegalGraph checks every new contract against it during first-pass review. Available on the Business and Enterprise plans.

Regulatory tracking

When a regulation changes, LegalGraph shows exactly which contracts and internal policies are affected. Coverage includes GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, the EU AI Act, DORA and major US federal and state privacy laws.

AI assistant

An AI chat assistant is available on every page of the app, so anyone on the team can ask a question without opening a ticket. Every answer includes source citations.

Audit-ready by default

Every answer, extraction and edit is logged with a source citation, so the general counsel can trust what shipped and prove it.

3. How it works

  1. Connect — upload contracts or connect your existing document store. No migration is required.
  2. Map — LegalGraph builds entities and relationships across every document and keeps them current automatically.
  3. Ask & act — research, review and report, with every answer explainable and cited back to its source.

Supported document formats: PDF (including scanned PDFs, via built-in OCR), Word (.docx) and plain text. Documents are typically searchable within 15 minutes of upload.

4. Integrations

LegalGraph connects to the document stores legal teams already use: iManage, NetDocuments, SharePoint, Google Drive, Box and Dropbox. Microsoft Word is supported through the LegalGraph add-in, which runs playbook checks while you draft. Single sign-on (SSO) is available through Okta, Microsoft Entra ID and Google Workspace on the Enterprise plan. A REST API is available on the Enterprise plan.

9. Frequently asked questions

How is LegalGraph different from a document search tool?

Search finds documents. LegalGraph maps the relationships between clauses, cases and regulations, so an answer can explain why — and show you the exact source it came from.

Can I use my existing document store?

Yes. Connect your existing repository or upload documents directly — no migration is required to get started.

Is my data used to train shared models?

No. Your documents and queries are never used to train models shared across customers.

Does the AI assistant replace legal judgment?

No. LegalGraph accelerates research and first-pass review with cited sources. Every output is meant to be checked by a licensed professional before it is relied on. LegalGraph does not provide legal advice.
